Who We Are

CS2-Services focuses on services and information connected with Counter-Strike 2, FACEIT, and Steam. The website describes its team as a group of experienced competitive players and boosters with more than 4,000 Elo experience. This competitive background informs the way we approach player goals, explain ranking milestones, and organise service information for people with different levels of experience in CS2.

We understand that players do not all come to the game with the same priorities. Some are focused on their FACEIT rating and level, others want to learn more about commend categories or improve the presentation of a Steam profile, while newer players may be looking for clear explanations of maps, aim practice, utility usage, match communication, or game settings. Our aim is to make these topics easier to explore from one central destination.

CS2-Services is built around a simple idea: useful information should be easy to find, service choices should be explained in plain language, and customers should be able to review the important details before deciding whether a service is right for them. We combine service pages with a growing collection of educational articles, platform profiles, and community resources.

What We Do

Our website is organised around three main service categories. Each category serves a different purpose, so visitors can go directly to the section that matches what they are researching.

1. FACEIT Elo, Level & Win Services

Our FACEIT section covers service options for players who have a specific Elo target, want to reach a particular FACEIT level, or are working toward a chosen number of wins. The service pages explain the available formats and give visitors a place to review target options and order details.

Depending on the selected service, options include Solo/Pilot and Duo/Lobby formats. These formats work differently: Solo/Pilot involves a booster playing on the customer's account, while Duo/Lobby involves the customer queueing and playing alongside a booster using their own account.

Explore FACEIT services · Elo boosting · Level boosting · Win boosting

2. CS2 Commend Services

CS2 commendations appear on player profiles in three categories: Friendly, Teacher, and Leader. Our commend service page presents available packages for players interested in these profile counters and explains the information needed to place an order.

For this category, customers are directed to use the relevant public Steam profile link. Commend counts should be understood as profile indicators; they are not a promise of higher Trust Factor, improved matchmaking, more wins, or better in-game performance.

3. Steam Profile Services

A Steam profile is one way players present their gaming identity and connect with other members of the community. Our Steam section covers profile comments, artwork and screenshot likes, friend-related services, and Steam group members.

Visitors can review the relevant service page to understand the available options and quantities. Service descriptions should be checked before ordering so the selected package matches the profile or community page they want to update.

Who We Serve

CS2-Services is intended for people who are active in the Counter-Strike 2 and Steam ecosystems. Our visitors include competitive players, casual players, profile owners, and readers who simply want to understand how different parts of the CS2 community work.

Competitive FACEIT Players

Players who follow the FACEIT ladder and want to learn about Elo targets, level milestones, win counts, and the differences between available boosting formats. The guides can also help readers understand how rating changes and match statistics are commonly discussed.

Players With Specific Progress Goals

Gamers who have a particular milestone in mind, such as a target rating, level, or number of wins. Our service pages provide a place to compare the relevant options and learn what each format involves.

Steam Profile Owners

Steam users interested in profile comments, artwork or screenshot likes, friend activity, and group participation. These services are separate from FACEIT rank and should be considered as profile or community-related options.

New and Returning CS2 Players

Players who want approachable material about aiming, warm-up routines, settings, map knowledge, buying rounds, team communication, utility, and common beginner mistakes.

Gaming Communities & Content Creators

Readers, creators, and community members who publish CS2 content, share guides, discuss competitive gaming, or follow updates and resources across different online platforms.

Players Comparing Their Options

People who prefer to research a service before making a decision. We encourage visitors to compare the terms, understand the risks, and use official platform information when checking rules or account restrictions.

Our Approach: Clear Information and Practical Resources

We believe that a good service experience starts with clear information. Before selecting a package, visitors should be able to understand what is included, how the process works, what account or profile information may be needed, and where to ask questions. We therefore organise our main service categories separately and link to supporting guides for visitors who want more background.

Service details before ordering

Service options can differ in how they are delivered. For example, the FACEIT Solo/Pilot option requires account access because a booster plays directly on the customer's account. The Duo/Lobby option does not require the customer to share login details because the customer plays on their own account alongside the booster. The current product page should always be treated as the source for the latest availability and conditions.

Public profile links for relevant Steam services

Steam profile-related orders generally use the public profile or content link relevant to the selected service. Customers should follow the instructions shown on the order page and should never send passwords, Steam Guard codes, recovery codes, or other confidential login information through an ordinary support conversation.

Learning beyond the service pages

Competitive improvement involves much more than a number on a profile. Good routines can include aim practice, crosshair and sensitivity settings, map knowledge, utility line-ups, communication, economy decisions, demo review, and learning from mistakes. Our content resources address these subjects so that players can explore the wider game as well as the services listed on the site.

Important FACEIT platform-policy notice

FACEIT's current Code of Conduct lists rank manipulation, boosting, and account sharing among prohibited behaviours. Third-party boosting may therefore lead to account or platform consequences. CS2-Services is independent and cannot guarantee that using a third-party service will avoid enforcement. Read the FACEIT Code of Conduct, the FACEIT Banning Policy, and our Terms of Service before making a decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CS2-Services?

CS2-Services is a website focused on Counter-Strike 2-related service options, including FACEIT Elo, level and win services, CS2 commend packages, and Steam profile services. It also shares guides and resources for players interested in the wider CS2 ecosystem.

Who are the services designed for?

The website is intended for CS2 and Steam users, including competitive FACEIT players, people researching progression milestones, Steam profile owners, and readers looking for CS2 guides and community resources.

Does every FACEIT service require account details?

No. The service format matters. Solo/Pilot involves a booster accessing and playing on the customer's account, while Duo/Lobby is played together with the customer on their own account. Review the current service page and consider FACEIT's rules before ordering.

Do CS2 commendations guarantee a higher Trust Factor?

No. Commend counts are profile indicators, and CS2-Services does not promise that commend services will increase Trust Factor, improve matchmaking, or change competitive results.

Is CS2-Services affiliated with Valve, Steam, or FACEIT?

No. CS2-Services is an independent website and is not affiliated with, associated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Valve Corporation, Steam, or FACEIT.

Where can I find service details and support?

Start with the relevant service page on the CS2-Services website. For order questions, use the contact details published on the site and check the Terms of Service before proceeding.
